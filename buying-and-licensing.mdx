## Buying

Products are sold on [teriyakigaming.com](https://teriyakigaming.com). Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy — you pay there, and the download links arrive in your order confirmation email right after purchase.

You don't need an account to buy. Your email address is your proof of purchase.

## Re-downloading your files

Lost the download link, or need the files on a new machine?

1. Go to Lemon Squeezy's [My Orders](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders) page.
2. Enter the email address you bought with. Lemon Squeezy emails you a sign-in link.
3. Open your order and download the latest files.

Updates are delivered the same way — when a product updates, your My Orders page always has the current version at no extra cost.
