## What to expect per pack

- A contents list — what's actually in the download, with counts.
- Supported Unreal Engine versions.
- Import and setup notes where a pack needs them.
- A changelog.

Until then, [teriyakigaming.com](https://teriyakigaming.com) is the place to watch for releases, and [buying and licensing](/buying-and-licensing) covers how purchases work.
